| Network | Who it's for | How to connect |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **[Corp-WiFi]** | Company laptops | Connects automatically |
| **[Guest-WiFi]** | Visitors and personal devices | Password posted at reception |

Company laptops should never be on the guest network. If your laptop doesn't connect to **[Corp-WiFi]** automatically, restart it while in the office. If that doesn't help, [contact the service desk](/support/get-help/).
