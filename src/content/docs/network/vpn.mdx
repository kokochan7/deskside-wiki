The VPN gives you secure access to internal systems when you're not in the office.

## Install the client

<Tabs syncKey="os">
	<TabItem label="Windows" icon="seti:windows">
		Open **[Software Center]**, search for **[VPN client]** and select **Install**.
	</TabItem>
	<TabItem label="macOS" icon="apple">
		Open **[Self Service]**, find **[VPN client]** and select **Install**.
	</TabItem>
</Tabs>

## Connect

<Steps>

1. Open **[VPN client]**.

2. Enter the server address **[vpn.example.com]** if it isn't filled in.

3. Sign in with your company account and approve the MFA prompt.

4. Wait for the status to show **Connected**.

</Steps>

## Troubleshooting

<Aside type="tip" title="Can't connect?">
Most VPN issues are fixed by these steps, in order.
</Aside>

- Check your internet works without the VPN by opening any public site.
- Quit and reopen the VPN client, then try again.
- Hotel and guest networks sometimes block VPNs. Try a phone hotspot.
- Restart your computer.

Still stuck? [Contact the service desk](/support/get-help/) with a screenshot of the error.
