## Forgot your password

<Steps>

1. Go to the self-service reset portal at **[password reset URL]**.

2. Enter your company email address.

3. Verify your identity with your MFA method.

4. Choose a new password that meets the [requirements](#password-requirements).

</Steps>

<Aside type="tip">
After resetting, your phone's email and Wi-Fi may keep asking for the old password. Update them with the new one to avoid being locked out again.
</Aside>

## Change a password you know

<Tabs syncKey="os">
	<TabItem label="Windows" icon="seti:windows">
		Press <kbd>Ctrl</kbd> + <kbd>Alt</kbd> + <kbd>Del</kbd> and choose **Change a password**.
	</TabItem>
	<TabItem label="macOS" icon="apple">
		Open **System Settings → Users & Groups**, select your account and choose **Change Password**.
	</TabItem>
	<TabItem label="Web">
		Sign in to **[account portal URL]** and go to **Security → Password**.
	</TabItem>
</Tabs>

## Password requirements

- At least **14 characters**. A passphrase of a few random words works well.
- Not used for any other site or service.
- Don't reuse your last 5 passwords.

## Locked out

Accounts lock after several failed attempts and unlock automatically after **[15] minutes**. If you need access sooner, [contact the service desk](/support/get-help/).
