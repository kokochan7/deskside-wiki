---
title: Request access to a system
description: How to ask for access to apps, shared drives and groups.
sidebar:
  order: 3
---

Access is granted on request and approved by the system owner or your manager.

1. Open a request in **[service desk portal]** under **Access request**.
2. Name the system, the level of access you need and why.
3. Your manager approves it, then IT grants access. Most requests are done within **[1 business day]**.

When you change teams, access from your old team is reviewed and removed where it's no longer needed.
