Multi-factor authentication (MFA) is required for all company accounts.

<Steps>

1. Install **[authenticator app]** on your phone from the App Store or Google Play.

2. On your computer, go to **[MFA enrollment URL]** and sign in.

3. Choose **Add method → Authenticator app** and scan the QR code with your phone.

4. Enter the 6-digit code shown in the app to confirm.

5. Add a **backup method**, such as a second device or a security key.

</Steps>

<Aside type="danger" title="Never share your codes">
Nobody from IT will ever ask for your MFA code. If someone does, report it.
</Aside>

## New or lost phone

If you still have the old phone, add the new phone as a method first, then remove the old one.

If the phone is lost, [contact the service desk](/support/get-help/) to reset your MFA. You'll need to verify your identity.
