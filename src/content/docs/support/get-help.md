## Contact the service desk

| Channel | Details | Best for |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Portal | **[service desk portal URL]** | Most requests |
| Email | **[it@example.com]** | When you can't reach the portal |
| Phone | **[phone number]** | Urgent issues, lockouts |
| Chat | **[#it-help]** | Quick questions |

Hours: **[Mon–Fri, 8:00–18:00]**.

## Write a good ticket

Include what you were trying to do, the exact error message (a screenshot is best), when it started, and what you've already tried.

## Urgent issues

Call for anything that stops you working entirely, or for any security incident such as a lost device or a suspected phishing click.
