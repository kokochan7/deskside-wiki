A short list of things everyone should do.

## Lock your screen

Lock your computer whenever you step away.

- **Windows:** <kbd>Win</kbd> + <kbd>L</kbd>
- **macOS:** <kbd>Ctrl</kbd> + <kbd>Cmd</kbd> + <kbd>Q</kbd>

## Spot phishing

Be suspicious of any email that:

- Creates urgency ("your account will be closed today")
- Asks for your password or MFA code. **IT will never ask for these.**
- Comes from a lookalike domain or an unexpected sender

If in doubt, don't click. Forward the email to [security reporting address].

## Keep devices updated

Install operating system and app updates when prompted. Restart at least once a week so updates can finish.

## Report incidents fast

Lost a laptop or clicked a suspicious link? Report it to IT immediately. Fast reporting matters more than anything else.
