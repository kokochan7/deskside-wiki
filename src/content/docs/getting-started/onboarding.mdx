## Before your first day

- Your manager requests your accounts at least **3 business days** before your start date.
- You'll receive a temporary password and a welcome email at your personal address.

## Day one setup

<Steps>

1. **Sign in to your laptop** with your company username and the temporary password you were given.

2. **Change your password** when prompted. See [password requirements](/accounts/password-reset/#password-requirements).

3. **Set up multi-factor authentication.** Follow [Set up MFA](/accounts/mfa/) before you sign in to anything else.

4. **Sign in to email and chat.** Open [email client] and [chat app] and sign in with your company account.

5. **Install the VPN** so you can work remotely. See [Connect to the VPN](/network/vpn/).

6. **Confirm your access.** Check that you can open the shared drives and apps your team uses. If something is missing, [request access](/accounts/request-access/).

</Steps>
