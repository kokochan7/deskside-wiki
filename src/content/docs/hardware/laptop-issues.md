## Try these first

- **Slow or frozen:** save your work and restart. Many problems disappear after a restart.
- **No sound or camera in calls:** check the app's device settings, then restart the app.
- **Won't charge:** try a different outlet and charger, and check the cable for damage.

## Lost, stolen or damaged

Report a lost or stolen laptop to IT **immediately** so it can be locked remotely. For damage, open a ticket and IT will arrange a repair or loaner.
